01

Introduction

Welcome to EkoSan, a Discord bot developed and maintained by Misad011 and the MisadTeam.

This Privacy Policy explains what information EkoSan collects, how that information is used, who can access it, and how it is stored and shared.

By using EkoSan, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully before using the bot.

02

Information We Collect

EkoSan’s !ask command uses a question-and-answer dataset to provide responses.

Questions submitted through !ask may be collected by the maintainer for consideration when expanding or improving EkoSan’s dataset.

Collected entries generally follow this JSON structure:

  • Q: The question submitted by the user.
  • A: The corresponding answer.

The !ask command prefix itself is not included in the stored question.

EkoSan’s question-and-answer collection does not intentionally include Discord usernames, Discord user IDs, or other Discord account identifiers.

Questions are not automatically published to the public database. The maintainer manually collects and adds selected question-and-answer entries to the official dataset.

Users should avoid submitting personal, confidential, or sensitive information because questions may be retained and could eventually be published.

03

Public Dataset and Data Access

EkoSan’s question-and-answer database is hosted in the following public GitHub repository:

github.com/Misad011/Ekosan-Database ↗

The repository is publicly readable. Consequently, anyone may be able to view, download, or copy its contents.

Members of the MisadTeam are authorized to access the collected question-and-answer data for purposes related to maintaining, reviewing, and improving EkoSan.

Only Misad011 has authorization to modify the official JSON files and database contents.

Although access to modify the official dataset is restricted, this does not prevent others from reading or copying publicly available information.

04

How We Use Collected Information

Collected questions and answers may be used to:

  • Consider new entries for EkoSan’s question-and-answer dataset.
  • Expand the range of questions EkoSan can answer.
  • Improve the usefulness and relevance of responses.
  • Review existing entries and identify errors or missing information.
  • Support the ongoing development and maintenance of EkoSan.

The maintainer, Misad011, manually decides which collected entries to add to the official database. Submission does not guarantee publication.

Once an entry is published, it becomes part of the publicly readable dataset hosted on GitHub. Anyone may be able to view, download, or copy published entries.

Only Misad011 can modify the official dataset.

05

Data Storage and Security

The question-and-answer dataset is stored in JSON format and hosted through GitHub.

We restrict modification of the official dataset to Misad011. Members of the MisadTeam may access the collected data as described in this policy.

However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Because the dataset is publicly accessible, information included in it should not be considered private.

We encourage users not to submit personal information, passwords, private conversations, or other confidential material.

06

Third-Party Services

EkoSan relies on third-party services, including Discord for bot functionality and GitHub for hosting its public dataset.

When using these services, users are also subject to the relevant providers’ own privacy policies and terms.

GitHub repository: Ekosan-Database ↗

EkoSan’s use of these services does not mean that Misad011 or the MisadTeam controls all data processing performed independently by those providers.

07

Data Retention and Removal

Questions and answers may remain in EkoSan’s dataset for as long as they are considered useful to the project.

Because the dataset is publicly accessible, third parties may copy or download its contents. Removing an entry from the official repository cannot guarantee the removal of copies held elsewhere.

If you believe an entry contains personal information or other information that should not have been published, contact the EkoSan maintainer through an official contact method provided by the project.

Requests will be reviewed, but removal cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance.

08

Children’s Privacy and Sensitive Information

Users should not submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information through EkoSan.

Do not use the bot to publish another person’s private information without authorization.

EkoSan’s safeguards and content-handling capabilities may change as development continues. Users should not assume that every inappropriate or sensitive submission will automatically be detected or blocked.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as EkoSan develops or its data-handling practices change.

When significant changes are made, the revised policy will be published at the project’s official Privacy Policy page.

Continued use of EkoSan after an updated policy takes effect will be subject to the applicable rules and requirements.
