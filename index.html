What can EkoSan do?

These are the commands currently available. Anything not listed as working is still being developed.

❔

Question answering

Ask EkoSan something with !ask. She searches her existing question-and-answer database, so answers may be incomplete or inaccurate.

✨

AI conversations

Use /ai to ask the AI a question or have a roleplay-style conversation. Messages are processed by NVIDIA; review the AI service terms before sharing anything.

🎨

Emoji importing

Import a ZIP of supported image and animation formats, and EkoSan will create Discord emojis, subject to Discord's limits and your server permissions.

💬

Send a message

Use /say to make EkoSan send a message in a channel where she has permission to do so.

Commands and capabilities may change as development continues.

·

Working commands

Here is the current command reference for EkoSan.

/emoji-import

Import emojis from a ZIP

Parameter: zip:

Upload a ZIP containing GIF, PNG, or JPEG files. EkoSan imports them as Discord emojis. The ZIP upload limit is 10 MB, and Discord's own emoji restrictions still apply.

/ai

Ask the AI or roleplay

Parameter: message:

Send a prompt to the AI and receive a generated response. Do not send private or sensitive information. NVIDIA's hosted API terms restrict certain content, including pornographic, offensive, hateful, harassing, violent, or threatening material.

!ask

Ask EkoSan from her database

Ask a question and EkoSan will look for an answer in her existing dataset. She can only answer what is represented in that database, and responses may not always be accurate.

/say

Send a message through EkoSan

Parameters: message: and channel:

Let EkoSan send the specified message in the selected channel, provided she has permission to view and send messages there.
